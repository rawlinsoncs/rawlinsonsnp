# Rawlinson SNP

The daily morning snack program at Rawlinson Community School: one snack per student per school day, planned from donated food with purchased gap-fill.

## Language

**Menu**:
The plan of which items make up each school day's Snack, published on the Snack Program Calendar.
_Avoid_: meal plan, snack schedule

**Snack**:
The daily serving: at least one vegetable or fruit plus at least one whole-grain, protein, or dairy item; met by distinct items or a single Dual-component item.
_Avoid_: breakfast, two-item snack

**Dual-component item**:
A single food that covers both Snack components, such as a whole wheat vegetable samosa.
_Avoid_: combo item, two-group item

**Substitution bag**:
A labeled individual bag replacing a Menu item for a student with an allergy or restriction; prepared day-of by the allergy system, independent of Menu planning.
_Avoid_: allergy menu, alternate snack

**Donation**:
Food supplied by a vendor on the vendor's own initiative; the program does not choose the contents.
_Avoid_: free delivery, subsidized order

**Gap-fill Purchase**:
An order of chosen items placed to complete school days whose Snack components are not covered by Donations and current stock.
_Avoid_: top-up order, make-up order

**Snack Program Calendar**:
The public calendar of planned snack distributions and vendor deliveries, embedded on the site's teacher guidance page; informational only, not a commitment.

**Donor**:
A vendor that supplies Donations on its own initiative; currently TFSS's Bridging the Nutrition Gap program.
_Avoid_: vendor (when the donation/purchase distinction matters)

**Week Cycle**:
A TFSS label for one scheduled Donation delivery; letters repeat (A, B, C, D…) with no fixed rotation.
_Avoid_: iteration, delivery week

**Supplier**:
A vendor the program places orders with for chosen items: Healthy Selections, Costco, Millennium Bakery.
_Avoid_: vendor (when the donation/purchase distinction matters)

**Distribution Volunteer**:
A volunteer on the daily morning snack distribution; the Menu's primary consumer.
_Avoid_: day-of volunteer, morning volunteer
