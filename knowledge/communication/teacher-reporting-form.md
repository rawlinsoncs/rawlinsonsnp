# Teacher Reporting Form

Teachers communicate operational changes through the [Teacher-specific Snack Program Reporting Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScRoEtHOad9SSLyxjuC_P0UjtdTEw9Yn8sncGmdXfXXrlgtTw/viewform?usp=header).[^teacher-form][^teacher-guidance]

## Reported Events

- Student dietary or allergy updates (feeding into the [Dietary Accommodations policy](../program/dietary-accommodations.md)).
- Class quantity adjustments or food waste feedback.
- Field trips / absences (requesting snacks to be sent early, held, or stored).

## Feedback Loop

Form submissions generate automated/manual email confirmations from volunteers to teachers.
