# Volunteer Workflows

## Onboarding and Sign-Up

- **Intake**: Volunteers sign up via the [Volunteer Intake Form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSc9vXzS8igf1ctnruGFHIYfP6KN_mPStFYAoPrZKV8o9Lds9g/viewform?usp=header).[^volunteer-intake]
- **Requirements**: Adult volunteers must complete a TDSB Police Reference Check.[^family-guide] High school students can participate to earn 40 hours of required community service.
- **Training**: Program coordinators complete formal food safety and student nutrition training. General volunteers receive on-the-job onboarding and do not require food handling certification.[^family-guide] Time commitment can be as little as 20 minutes per week.
- Coordination happens via the [WhatsApp group](../communication/whatsapp-coordination.md).

## Daily Morning Snack Distribution (~8:20 AM – 9:15 AM)

[^family-guide]

1. **8:20 AM Arrival & Bin Setup**: Volunteers arrive at the main floor snack tables near the office. Previous day's bins are retrieved, sanitized, and laid out in order by classroom. Missing bins are replaced.
2. **Snack Retrieval & Prep**: Food is fetched from the 2nd-floor pantry or main floor lunchroom refrigerators. On fruit/vegetable days, produce is washed or portioned by weight.
3. **Portioning by Classroom Count**: Items are counted out according to classroom lists (e.g., an apple and a cheese string per student, or yogurt cup + spoon per student).
4. **Dietary Substitution Bagging**: Labeled substitution bags are added to specific classroom bins per the [Dietary Accommodations policy](../program/dietary-accommodations.md).
5. **Bin Delivery**: Kindergarten bins are delivered directly to kindergarten classrooms; Grade 1–8 bins are placed on tables outside the main office by **9:15 AM** for teacher pickup.
6. **Cleanup**: Cardboard boxes are broken down for recycling, and the prep area is reset.

## Moving Food Deliveries

A separate role from morning distribution, handling heavy cases (up to ~45 lbs) from the main floor entry to storage locations.[^family-guide] Storage destinations and vendor schedules are in [Pantry and Inventory](./pantry-inventory.md).
