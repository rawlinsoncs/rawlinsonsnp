# Dietary Accommodations and Allergy Policy

## Accommodations

Accommodates students with allergies, medical restrictions, and religious observances (e.g., halal) by placing clearly labeled individual substitution bags inside designated classroom snack bins.[^family-guide][^teacher-guidance]

## Strict Verification Policy

Allergy and dietary accommodations must be submitted in writing through the school office or via official forms. Verbal requests from parents or students cannot lift or modify allergy protocols.[^family-guide][^teacher-guidance]

Anaphylactic classroom bins are kept separate and sanitized daily, with allergen checklists attached.

Teachers report dietary updates via the [Teacher Reporting Form](../communication/teacher-reporting-form.md).
