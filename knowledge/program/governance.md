# Governance and Partners

## Volunteer-Led

With the exception of school administration support (the principal, office staff, and a teacher liaison), the program is run entirely by parent and community volunteers.[^family-guide][^teacher-guidance] Volunteer roles encompass coordination, packing, delivery moving, dietary substitution management, and menu planning. See [Volunteer Workflows](../operations/volunteer-workflows.md).

## Institutional Governance

- **Toronto District School Board (TDSB)**: Oversees nutrition policy. TDSB Nutrition Liaison Officer Lynne Martens (`lynne.martens@tdsb.on.ca`) provides liaison support.[^family-guide]
- **Toronto Public Health (TPH)**: Public Health Inspectors conduct annual inspections of food storage and prep spaces; TPH Registered Dietitians review menus to ensure compliance with [Nutrition Standards](./nutrition-standards.md).[^family-guide]

## Funding & Supply Partners

- Toronto Foundation for Student Success (TFSS)
- Healthy Selections
- Millennium Bakery
- Costco Business Delivery

Vendor delivery schedules are covered in [Pantry and Inventory](../operations/pantry-inventory.md).
