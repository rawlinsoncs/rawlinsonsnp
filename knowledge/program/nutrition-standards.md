# Nutrition Standards

The authoritative owner of these rules is the Ontario Ministry of Children, Community and Social Services *Student Nutrition Program — Nutrition Guidelines 2020*[^mcsss-guidelines]; the City of Toronto/TPH pages are adaptations of it and agree on every threshold.[^toronto-guidelines] Full extraction with per-category serve / do-not-serve lists: [TDSB SNP Product Choice Requirements](../research/tdsb-snp-product-requirements.md).

## Snack structure

A snack includes at least one **vegetable or fruit** plus one **protein food OR whole grain food**; dairy counts as a protein food under Canada's Food Guide 2019.[^mcsss-guidelines][^teacher-guidance] See [Program Overview](./overview.md).

## Numeric criteria

| Rule | Criterion | Scope |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Sugar | ≤ 8 g per 30 g serving | Whole grain products (pro-rate when the label's stated serving differs from 30 g) |
| Sugar | ≤ 11 g per 100 g serving | Flavoured yogurt |
| Sodium | ≤ 10% DV per stated serving | ALL categories |
| Whole grain | whole grain, whole wheat, or **bran** first on the ingredient list | Whole grain products (in addition to the sugar and sodium criteria) |
| Yogurt milk fat | ≤ 2% MF | Yogurt, kefir, soy yogurt |
| Cheese milk fat | ≤ 20% MF when possible | Cheese |
| Milk | skim / 1% / 2% only | Milk |

## Hard prohibitions (regardless of label)

- All vegetable and fruit juice, including 100% juice[^toronto-guidelines]
- Flavoured milk, hot chocolate, 3.25% (homogenized) milk[^mcsss-section3]
- Candy, chocolate, frozen treats, pudding; foods with sugar substitutes or artificial trans fat[^mcsss-section3]
- "Do not serve" items are always do-not-serve — a passing Nutrition Facts table does not rescue them[^toronto-guidelines]

Nut and seed choices must follow the school anaphylaxis policy.[^mcsss-section3] The provincial guidelines were under review as of 2026 — recheck before encoding thresholds in automation.[^mcsss-guidelines]
