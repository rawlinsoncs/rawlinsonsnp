# Nutrition Standards

Servings must strictly adhere to TDSB and City of Toronto guidelines regarding sugar and sodium limits:[^teacher-guidance]

- Whole grain items must list whole grain/wheat first in ingredients
- Sugar: ≤ 8 g per 30 g serving
- Sodium: ≤ 10% Daily Value

Each snack includes two components — see [Program Overview](./overview.md).
