# Rawlinson Student Nutrition Program (SNP)

The Rawlinson SNP provides a daily morning snack to every student at Rawlinson Community School, serving approximately **720 students** across elementary and kindergarten classrooms, five days a week.[^family-guide]

## Universal Access Model

Snacks are offered to every student regardless of whether an opt-in or permission form has been submitted by a family.[^family-guide][^teacher-guidance] This approach guarantees equitable access to food, reduces classroom hunger barriers, and removes stigma surrounding student nutrition programs.

## Snack Structure

Operates as an **AM Snack** program. Each snack includes two components following TDSB and City of Toronto Student Nutrition Program Guidelines (2021):[^family-guide]

1. *Vegetables or fruit* (e.g., fresh apples, seasonal produce).
2. *Whole grain, protein, or dairy/alternative item* (e.g., cheese strings, yogurt tubes/cups with disposable spoons, breadsticks with WOW butter, or whole grain baked goods).

See [Nutrition Standards](./nutrition-standards.md) for serving rules.

## No Kitchen Constraint

Rawlinson lacks a food preparation kitchen and food handling certifications, so all items are pre-packaged or minimal-prep.[^family-guide]
