The sugar threshold is ≤ 8 g **per 30 g serving**. The label states a serving of 35 g, so sugar is pro-rated:

> Sugar per 30 g = (sugar per 35 g) × 30 / 35

## Two-component snack role

Each passing flavour (Apple Crumble, Berry Vanilla) can serve as the **whole grain component** of the AM Snack. Per the two-component rule, it must be paired with a vegetable or fruit component (e.g., fresh fruit from a TFSS Donation).[^mccss][^local-criteria]

## Data provenance and caveats

- **Ingredient lists and Nutrition Facts** were obtained from both well.ca (retailer, Apple Crumble only)[^wellca] and madegoodfoods.ca (manufacturer, all three flavours)[^mg-apple][^mg-berry][^mg-banana]. The ingredient lists match between retailer and manufacturer for Apple Crumble, confirming data consistency.
- **well.ca stocks Apple Crumble only** (5-pack, $6.99, Final Sale). Berry Vanilla and Banana Chocolate Chip were assessed from the manufacturer's Canadian site only; availability at well.ca or other Suppliers (Healthy Selections, Costco) should be confirmed by the coordinator.
- **Banana Chocolate Chip is a hard Do Not Serve** due to chocolate chips, regardless of passing numeric criteria. If a variety pack is ordered that includes this flavour, it must be excluded from the Menu or given only to students whose caregivers have specifically authorized it (not as part of the standard SNP Menu).
- **Label verification before ordering**: Per the procurement rule, the coordinator must check physical package labels before ordering, as product formulations can change.[^local-criteria]
