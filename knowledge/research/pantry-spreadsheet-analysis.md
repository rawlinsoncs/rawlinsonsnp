### Sheet details

A read-only computed view. Column A uses `QUERY({AvailableBreakdown!A2:B1000, AvailableBreakdown!C2:C1000}, ...)` to pull item, category, and available quantity from the breakdown sheet. The `__xludf.DUMMYFUNCTION("COMPUTED_VALUE")` wrapper is a Google Sheets artifact indicating this is a computed value not available in static export.

Headers are implicit (no header row — data starts at row 1). Table `Table_3` covers `A1:Z1000` with generic column names `Column1` through `Column15`.

#### 2. AvailableAsOf

Time-sliced inventory. Row 1 has a parameter cell I1 labeled "As of date:" with a date value (serial 46197 = 2026-06-24 in xlsx; "2026-06-24" in ods). ("AvailableAsOf" sheet, I1, Pantry (5).xlsx)

Headers: `Item | Category | Available as of | Deliveries | Distributions | Waste` — then columns G–I are: blank, blank, "As of date:" cutoff. ("AvailableAsOf" sheet, A1:I1, Pantry (5).xlsx)

Formulas (per data row, e.g., row 2):
- **C** (Available as of): `$D2-$E2-$F2` (deliveries minus distributions minus waste up to cutoff)
- **D** (Deliveries): `LET(item, $A2, cutoff, $I$1, delivs, SUMIFS(Deliveries!$G:$G, Deliveries!$A:$A, item, Deliveries!$J:$J, "<="&cutoff), delivs)` — sums ServingsProjected from Deliveries where date ≤ cutoff
- **E** (Distributions): `LET(item, $A2, cutoff, $I$1, outs, MAX(0, SUMIFS(Distributions!$C:$C, Distributions!$B:$B, item, Distributions!$A:$A, "<="&cutoff)), outs)` — sums QtyOut from Distributions
- **F** (Waste): Same pattern, SUMIFS against Waste!$C:$C

## Process insights

From the spreadsheet structure, the manual workflow appears to be:

1. **Receiving**: When a delivery arrives, a row is added to the Deliveries sheet with item name, unit (servings per pack), pack size (number of packs/cases), vendor, delivery date, and optional notes. ServingsReceived is auto-calculated from Unit×PackSize but only counts if the date is in the past.
2. **Distributing**: When items are given to students, a row is added to the Distributions sheet with date, item, and quantity distributed.
3. **Waste tracking**: Expired/damaged items are logged in the Waste sheet with quantity and reason (many entries lack dates, suggesting they were backfilled or estimated).
4. **Inventory view**: AvailableBreakdown auto-aggregates all three sheets. Available and AvailableAsOf provide computed views.
5. **Spending tracking**: HealthySelections orders are tracked separately with order IDs and dollar amounts.
6. **Vendor analysis**: A summary pivot shows servings by vendor and category.

The "Rebalance" entries in Deliveries (rows 132, 138) suggest periodic inventory adjustments are made directly in the Deliveries sheet rather than through a separate mechanism. The "Inventory" vendor (14 deliveries) appears to be used for internal stock adjustments.

## Open questions

These questions need answers for automation design:

1. **Who enters data?** Is it one person or multiple? Do they use the Google Sheets UI directly, or import CSVs?
2. **What is "A1" vendor?** It appears only on 2025-09-29 with 4 deliveries. Is this a one-time vendor, a code, or an error?
3. **What triggers "Inventory" entries?** 14 deliveries from "Inventory" vendor between Feb–Apr 2026 — are these physical inventory counts, transfers between floors, or corrections?
4. **Why are most Waste entries undated?** Only 4 of ~50 waste entries have dates. How does the coordinator decide when to log waste?
5. **What does "Spent" column track?** Column E in Deliveries has values for some rows but not others. Is this dollar amount? Why only for some vendors?
